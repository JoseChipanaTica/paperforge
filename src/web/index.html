Paperforge calls Claude with your key. It's kept only in this browser, sent with each request, and never saved or logged on our server.

Every paper has a machine inside.

Paste an arXiv link. Claude reads the method section, and the architecture builds itself in 3D, every block tied to the sentence it came from.

How it works

  1. Paste a link

    Any arXiv paper that proposes a model: a link, or just its id.

  2. Claude reads the method

    It pulls out every block with its equation, tensor shapes and the exact sentence that defines it.

  3. It builds and teaches

    The model assembles in 3D. Then follow an example through it, step by step, or read the PyTorch code.
